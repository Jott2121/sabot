# Vorhees-Antilla Field Services — Rotating Equipment Inspection Report

**Document control:** VAFS-RIR / Revision C (supersedes Revisions A and B)
**Facility:** Marrable Basin Lift Station No. 7, Sublevel D
**Report class:** Quarterly condition-monitoring, Tier 2 vibration survey

---

## 1. Asset identification

The asset under inspection is the No. 3 raw-water transfer pump, factory designation
**Grendon-Slaithe HX-4180**, plant tag **P-3117-D**. For the purposes of the plant
asset register the controlling unit identifier is the plant tag; the factory
designation is retained only for spare-parts cross-referencing. Unit identifier of
record: **P-3117-D**.

This pump was recommissioned after the Revision-B teardown and has accumulated 2,140
running hours since the seal replacement.

## 2. Inspection schedule

The on-site condition survey was carried out on **14 March 2029** by the assigned
rotating-equipment analyst. Per the Tier 2 survey interval (183 days), the next
scheduled condition survey falls due **13 September 2029**. Both dates are recorded in
the plant CMMS against work order WO-55902.

## 3. Measured readings

Three primary condition indicators were captured during the survey.

**3.1 Discharge-side manifold pressure.** Steady-state discharge manifold pressure was
logged at **128.4 psi** at rated speed, within the 120–135 psi acceptance window.

**3.2 Drive-end bearing vibration (broadband velocity).** The drive-end bearing
broadband vibration was initially transcribed from the analyzer at 47.1 µm RMS. During
report review it was found that the analyzer had been left on the wrong integration
window for the first sweep; the value was re-derived on the correct window. **The
body figure of 47.1 µm below is superseded — see the Figure 2 caption for the
corrected value, which governs.**

**3.3 Bearing housing temperature.** Drive-end bearing housing temperature stabilized
at **63.9 °C** after 40 minutes at rated load, below the 80 °C alarm threshold.

## 4. Analyst findings and disposition

No new bearing defect frequencies were observed on the corrected spectrum. All three
governing indicators — 128.4 psi discharge, 41.7 µm corrected drive-end vibration, and
63.9 °C bearing housing temperature — fall inside their acceptance windows. The analyst
therefore clears the asset to continue in normal service with no restriction, pending
the next scheduled Tier 2 survey.

**Disposition: RETURN TO SERVICE.**
